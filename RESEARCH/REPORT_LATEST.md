## Round 008 highlights — Mondrian-ACI hybrid

**What landed**: `aci_mondrian_step` + `aci_mondrian_stream` in `src/conformal.py`. Per-regime q_t with the same online update rule as plain ACI; only the active regime's q is updated per step. 9 new tests including a **bit-exact reduction** check: with one regime label, Mondrian-ACI is identical to `aci_stream` (load-bearing).

**Mondrian-ACI's max |gap| anywhere is 0.54pp.** Plain ACI is ±5–10pp. Mondrian-LAC is ±3–8pp. **Mondrian-ACI beats both.**

**Closes round-002's canonical target**: round-002 LEDGER named the α=0.20 low-vol p_online gap (−7.9pp) as "the gap H-202..H-208 must close." Mondrian-ACI closes it to +0.04pp.

**Why it works**: each regime maintains its own q_t with its own per-step update. Per-regime score distributions drift independently with volatility regime; the static batch Mondrian-LAC q_hat can't track that drift, but online per-regime ACI can. The per-regime q-trajectory plot shows low-vol q staying low and high-vol q staying high, exactly as expected.

**Marginal coverage stays on target**: 0.9004 / 0.8998 / 0.8015 at α=0.10 / 0.10 / 0.20 (within 0.002 of target).

**Verdict**: APPROVE. The online conformal layer's headline metric (per-regime coverage gap) is now solved at the basic-stream level; remaining online refinements (H-204 estimator-comparison, H-207 ADWIN drift reset, H-208 trade-gate) are incremental on top of this.

## Round 005 highlights — regime-stratified calibration

**What landed**: four helpers in `src/utils.py` ported from sibling — `expected_calibration_error`, `compute_all_metrics`, `calibration_by_regime`, `threshold_analysis`. 13 new tests pin invariants (ECE=0 on perfect calibration, ECE=|p−base_rate| on constant predictor, p=1.0 in last bin, regime n_samples sum to N, etc.). **Key empirical finding** (on test slice, n=31,486, parkinson_var_rolling_mean_24 terciles):

| regime | n | base_rate | offline ECE | offline mean_p | online ECE | online mean_p |
|--------|------|-----------|-------------|----------------|------------|---------------|
| low    | 10,495 | 0.022 | **0.050** | 0.072 (3.3× over) | 0.015 | 0.037 |
| med    | 10,495 | 0.074 | **0.103** | 0.177 (2.4× over) | 0.014 | 0.088 |
| high   | 10,496 | 0.195 | **0.171** | 0.366 (1.9× over) | 0.016 | 0.199 |

Offline calibration breaks worst at high volatility (ECE 0.17, mean_p nearly 2× base rate); online ARF achieves regime-flat ECE ≈ 0.015. This is direct empirical proof of CONSTITUTION I's claim — the online layer trades ranking power for regime-conditional calibration. Diagnostic [`calibration_by_regime.png`](RESEARCH/diagrams/round_005/calibration_by_regime.png) shows the curves visually: top row (offline) systematically below diagonal in every regime; bottom row (online) on the diagonal in every regime.

**Implication for H-202..H-208**: any conformal-coverage improvement should target the high-vol regime first (largest ECE gap on offline) — that's where the conditional miscalibration lives.

**Verdict**: APPROVE.

## Architecture context (unchanged from round 001)

### Two-stage barrier classifier

- **Decision interval**: M = 20 minutes.
- **Label**: `y_k = 1[ ln(H_{k+1} / C_k) ≥ α ]`, α = 0.0041113 (90th percentile of train log-excursions).
- **Splits**: chronological, `train_fraction=0.6`, `val_fraction (of train)=0.2`. No embargo (H-104 open).
- **Burn-in**: 96 bars per segment.

### Legacy reported results (floor)

- Test n = 31,486; positive rate = 0.0971.
- Offline: ROC=0.813, PR=0.356, Brier=0.090, mean p̂=0.205 (over-predicts).
- Online (final): ROC=0.799, PR=0.331, Brier=0.076 (~16% better), mean p̂≈base rate.
- First PnL (round 001): offline τ=0.30 PSR=0.995; online has no Sharpe edge by design.

### Primary metrics by stage

- **Online-stage (H-201..H-208)**: marginal + per-regime coverage at α ∈ {0.05, 0.10, 0.20}; tightness; coverage gap by volatility tercile.
- **Offline-stage**: BSS = 1 − Brier_model / Brier_base_rate, log-loss, ECE.
- **Backtest-tagged**: deflated Sharpe + bootstrap p_value + max DD + CDaR(5%).
