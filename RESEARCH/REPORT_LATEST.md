## Round 005 highlights — regime-stratified calibration

**What landed**: four helpers in `src/utils.py` ported from sibling — `expected_calibration_error`, `compute_all_metrics`, `calibration_by_regime`, `threshold_analysis`. 13 new tests pin invariants (ECE=0 on perfect calibration, ECE=|p−base_rate| on constant predictor, p=1.0 in last bin, regime n_samples sum to N, etc.). Latent bug fixed: `src/training_helpers.py:204` referenced `compute_all_metrics` which didn't exist on this project.

**Key empirical finding** (on test slice, n=31,486, parkinson_var_rolling_mean_24 terciles):

| regime | n | base_rate | offline ECE | offline mean_p | online ECE | online mean_p |
|--------|------|-----------|-------------|----------------|------------|---------------|
| low    | 10,495 | 0.022 | **0.050** | 0.072 (3.3× over) | 0.015 | 0.037 |
| med    | 10,495 | 0.074 | **0.103** | 0.177 (2.4× over) | 0.014 | 0.088 |
| high   | 10,496 | 0.195 | **0.171** | 0.366 (1.9× over) | 0.016 | 0.199 |

Offline calibration breaks worst at high volatility (ECE 0.17, mean_p nearly 2× base rate); online ARF achieves regime-flat ECE ≈ 0.015. This is direct empirical proof of CONSTITUTION I's claim — the online layer trades ranking power for regime-conditional calibration. Diagnostic [`calibration_by_regime.png`](RESEARCH/diagrams/round_005/calibration_by_regime.png) shows the curves visually: top row (offline) systematically below diagonal in every regime; bottom row (online) on the diagonal in every regime.

**Implication for H-202..H-208**: any conformal-coverage improvement should target the high-vol regime first (largest ECE gap on offline) — that's where the conditional miscalibration lives.

**Verdict**: APPROVE.

## Architecture context (unchanged from round 001)

### Two-stage barrier classifier

- **Decision interval**: M = 20 minutes.
- **Label**: `y_k = 1[ ln(H_{k+1} / C_k) ≥ α ]`, α = 0.0041113 (90th percentile of train log-excursions).
- **Splits**: chronological, `train_fraction=0.6`, `val_fraction (of train)=0.2`. No embargo (H-104 open).
- **Burn-in**: 96 bars per segment.

- Test n = 31,486; positive rate = 0.0971.
- Offline: ROC=0.813, PR=0.356, Brier=0.090, mean p̂=0.205 (over-predicts).
- Online (final): ROC=0.799, PR=0.331, Brier=0.076 (~16% better), mean p̂≈base rate.

### Primary metrics by stage

- **Online-stage (H-201..H-208)**: marginal + per-regime coverage at α ∈ {0.05, 0.10, 0.20}; tightness; coverage gap by volatility tercile.
- **Offline-stage**: BSS = 1 − Brier_model / Brier_base_rate, log-loss, ECE.
- **Backtest-tagged**: deflated Sharpe + bootstrap p_value + max DD + CDaR(5%).
